# Rung 0 — the replicate ceiling

**Task** `rung0-replicate-ceiling` · **Status** OPEN — promoted; closes at merge · **Branch** `rung0-replicate-ceiling`
**Steps** build, restrict, split, score, null, promote, document.
**Spec** [docs/SPEC.md](../../SPEC.md), rung 0 · **State** [docs/STATE.md](../../STATE.md)
**As of** 2026-08-27.

## What this rung establishes

How much of a measured perturbation response is signal rather than assay noise.
Every higher rung's score is read as a fraction of this ceiling: a 0.2 against a ceiling of 0.2 sits at the limit the assay supports, while the same 0.2 against 0.9 is a large shortfall.
Without this number, every rung above reports against an implicit 1.0 and a weak result cannot be told apart from an unmeasurable target.

## The measured quantity — and what it is not

The delta for a (line, drug) pair is its **differential expression profile**: the per-gene log2 fold change of treated vs DMSO from the Tahoe-100M pseudobulk DE table.
Replicate plates are split deterministically by `hash(plate) % 2`; each half's per-gene fold changes are averaged; the two half-profiles are correlated across the declared gene panel.
One correlation per (line, drug) pair — the delta's own test-retest reliability.

Two neighbouring quantities are deliberately **not** the measure:

- **Mean-expression reproducibility** (correlating half-pool expression levels) tracks assay stability of abundance, not response reproducibility. It comes out near 1 on the back of housekeeping-vs-low-expressor structure both halves share regardless of perturbation, and no rung predicts it. That shared structure belongs in the null.
- **Per-gene reliability** (the transpose: each gene's delta correlated across pairs between halves) says *which* panel genes carry reproducible perturbation signal. It is computed in the same run as an **unpromoted diagnostic** — a CSV and figure in this task folder, referenced from `verification.md` — because it is where to look as the evidence base for any future panel restriction. It carries no promoted claim; a task that wants to cite it promotes it with its own controls.

## The declared statistic (ladder-wide)

Per-pair **Pearson** correlation over the declared panel, aggregated as the **mean over pairs**, with the Spearman-Brown lift `2r/(1+r)` to full-data reliability stated wherever used.
Every delta rung reports this same statistic; the declaration lives in `docs/SPEC.md`'s rung 0 and rung 1 measure lines.
This supersedes the old-lineage median headline — see the decision history.

  **What this resolves to, measured.** It is not 32 × 50: the 32 CIDs resolve to 33 Tahoe drug names — Trametinib appears additionally as its `DMSO_TF` solvate, `Trametinib (DMSO_TF solvate)` — giving 1,650 candidate (line, drug) conditions. The Trametinib-vs-solvate mismatches (different line, one underlying compound, two Tahoe drug names) count toward the diff-drug null despite being one compound: a conservative placement, since it can only raise that floor, affecting roughly 2,500 of the diff-drug stratum's ~2.55M candidate mismatch pairs.

## Why use the DE statistics as published

The `log2FoldChange` values are used exactly as published — no re-quantification from the raw cells, no re-normalization, no refitting of the differential-expression model. That acceptance is a decision, made here:

- **The ceiling must be measured on the quantity the ladder actually uses.** The delta rungs consume this published table; re-deriving DE would make rung 0 the ceiling of a target nothing above it reads.
- **The published contrast is the better-informed one.** The producers computed the plate-matched treated-vs-DMSO contrast with full access to plate and well structure; recomputing from the ~100M raw cells is a multi-terabyte pipeline whose own analytic choices would then need their own validation, without testing any question the ladder asks.
- **The acceptance is pinned and checkable, not blind.** The tranche fixes the exact revision and content hash, so "upstream" cannot silently change; the split-half measurement itself would expose a table without genuine replicate structure; and the per-gene diagnostic flags genes whose published statistics fail to reproduce across plate halves.

What would revisit it: a rung needing a quantity the published table cannot supply (raw counts, per-cell resolution), or per-gene diagnostics implicating the upstream processing rather than the assay. A revisit is a dated reversal here, per project rule 2.

## Controls and power (project rule 4)

- **build** — positive: a synthetic replicate pool with planted structure flows through the real DuckDB split-half builder and comes out with the planted shape (expected pair count, both halves populated). Negative: a pool with no plate replication yields no scoreable pairs.
- **restrict** — positive: a planted panel-file subset restricts scoring to exactly those genes (new test below); negative: a panel disjoint from the data's genes aborts the run rather than scoring an empty panel (new test below); the promotion-time sha256 of the gene and drug panel files pins what was restricted in the promoted record.
- **split** — positive and negative are the existing build controls (a planted pool splits into two populated halves — `test_build_positive_planted_pool_comes_out_with_the_planted_shape`; a single plate cannot split — `test_build_negative_no_replication_yields_no_scoreable_pairs`).
- **score** — positive: a planted reliability of known value (shared per-pair signal plus independent half-noise, expected r in closed form) is recovered within tolerance by the real scoring function. Negative: planted zero signal returns null (p > 0.05).
- **null** — positive: planting separate drug-shared and line-specific components recovers the ordering observed > same-drug null > diff-drug null. Negative: on signal-free data the stratified nulls sit at their floors and the observed clears neither.
- **Empirical in-run control** — pairs stratified into effect-size terciles by mean |delta|; split-half mean r must rise monotonically across terciles (reported as CSV columns). An assay that cannot find more reproducibility where there is more signal is broken.
- **Power** — every promoted comparison reports its minimum detectable effect at α = 0.05, power = 0.80, from `minimum_detectable_aggregate` (the same null bootstrap as the p-value, read in the other direction). Positive-control plants sit at roughly 2× the MDE. Expected here: trivially powered (n ≈ 1,600 pairs, SE of the mean ≈ 0.002 from the pilot's spread) — and reporting that is the point, because the same columns will report honestly at rung 5 where power is the whole question.

## Statistics machinery

`src/fmharness/statistics.py`: `bootstrap_aggregate_pvalue` (ported — an aggregate is compared against the bootstrapped sampling distribution of the null *aggregate* at the observed pair count, never against the spread of individual null draws; the per-item form inflates p by orders of magnitude and is pinned by a known-answer test) and `minimum_detectable_aggregate` (new). Both carry known-answer tests importing the real functions.

## Why three null strata

A split-half correlation has a nonzero floor because genes share structure regardless of perturbation, and a single mixed null conflates two different floors (the archived lineage measured this: a mixed null came back at median 0.139 with 23% of draws above the observed, inflated by same-drug matches).
`any_pair` is reported for continuity; `diff_drug` (different line and drug) is the generic-structure floor the ceiling must clear to be a ceiling at all; `same_drug` (same drug, different line) is the line-specificity floor a pair must beat for its delta to be reproducible beyond its drug's average effect.
